The clauses

Role-anchored (the gate): each role stays near its own authored hue. Vocabulary membership (the migration clause): each hue stays near some authored hue, which is what permits role permutation — the mechanical definition of “transformative”. Relational (the signature): the authored pairwise separations hold.

The relational clause carries most of the meaning. The authored dark face has primary and warmAccent only 4.55° apart — a tight violet pair with a gold counterpoint. That single tight relation is Nebula's chromatic signature, and it is the first thing an alignment destroys (dH_PR 4.5 → 79.3).

A note on why the pairwise test alone is not enough: dH_PQ survives the counterfactual (3.8° off) while dH_PR breaks by 74.7°. Testing the relations as a set would have missed it; the tight pair is what carries the signal.
