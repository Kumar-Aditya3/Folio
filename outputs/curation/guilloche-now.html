The guilloche as it renders now — Abyss dark, 3×

Not fixed. The striation resolves at the flanks, but the body is still a solid pale wash, and the form is a flat ribbon rather than a flowing one.

Measured across the band: coverage 0.38–0.66 with 14–19% gaps. A filament field needs low coverage and a high gap fraction; 0.66 coverage with 14% gaps is still a solid band.

The density is concentrated at the crossing: the primary's 26 threads and the secondary's 14 overlap there, so 40 threads share the widest part of the ribbon — and the crossing boost multiplies the alpha 1.5× on top. That is why the flanks striate and the middle does not.
