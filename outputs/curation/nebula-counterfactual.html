Nebula: the counterfactual, not a repair

Nebula is the one theme the structural gates cannot settle. Forest had a demonstrable erroneous mapping (15.8° → 105.4°) correctable while restoring the light face's relationships. Nebula has no such defect, so the question is a design one — does restoring structural correspondence make Nebula better, or destroy what makes it Nebula?

What the same machinery would do

Alignment raises T6 58 → 89 and W6 7 → 20. That is not a marginal gain: T6 89 is above the Class P gate of 70. The aligned version would pass the structural gate Nebula currently fails.

And the hues it has to move are the identity: primaryAccent 312° → 122° (violet → green), secondaryAccent 52° → 226° (gold → blue), warmAccent 317° → 43°.

That is the whole argument in one line: the optimizer would score this as a pass, and the theme would no longer be Nebula. Metric improvement does not imply design improvement — and here the metric does not merely improve, it succeeds, while the design fails.

Not applied

The gates were not changed for Nebula, and T6 ≥ 70 was not quietly rewritten as “except Nebula”. The theme class is decided first: Nebula is Class X, held to a semantic envelope derived from its own authored swatches. This counterfactual is kept permanently as a negative control — it is the concrete demonstration that a future optimisation pass could otherwise rediscover this failure and call it a success.
