Colour-system audit — the 18 faces against the brief's bars

Measured, not eyeballed. Every rule in the colour brief is a number, so this runs them as assertions over the sheet's authored hexes. 25 checks fail across 18 faces, down from 31, in three patterns.

After the audit pass — 25 failing checks, was 31

Both headline categories are gone. No face now has d(hP,hQ) < 90°, and no face has C(R) > C(P). The dark L(B0), L(S0) and ladder failures are gone too.

How: the first two were assignment problems, not colour problems, so assign.py now picks P as the most chromatic swatch, Q as the one furthest from it in hue, and R as the most chromatic of the rest — with the leftover landing on the warm note so all six still appear. Every hex remains the sheet's own. The dark pages were placed at the brief's lightness with their authored hue kept.

What remains is one systematic contradiction in the brief — it requires both "P is the most chromatic" and C(X) ≥ C(P), which can only hold if X is P — plus five bridge-separation cases and two nebula surface values.
