# Between chapters

Folio's companion is a living paper bookmark. It uses the app's current paper,
ink and accent colours rather than a fixed animal illustration or room palette.
This replaces the Sable nook direction in the earlier design notes.

The bookmark sits at the left edge of the current cover in both Home layouts and Between chapters. Tapping it opens
**Between chapters**; the labelled link offers the same action. On the destination,
the current cover and bookmark share one paper surface. The cover returns to the
book; the bookmark opens its saved passage. Games and the reading journal use
Folio's existing type, materials and navigation language.

Small paper tilts and blinks provide expression. Holding uncurls the loose edge.
Reduced motion, hidden windows, covered Home and viewport visibility stop motion.
The character is drawn natively, including the fold and paper markings, so it can
adapt to every theme without recolouring a bitmap. Existing decoration purchases
map to linen, night ink, fern, ruled and star bookmark styles.

The paper now has curved sides, a lifted corner and a flexible loose tip. Gaze,
brows, eyelids and mouth change together for curiosity, reading, delight,
surprise, an uncurled stretch and sleep. Quiet idle glances and double blinks
alternate with rest; touch and game events interrupt them immediately. Motion
is read during drawing so the surrounding Home layout does not recompose on
each animation frame. Reduced motion retains expressive still poses.

Copy describes actions and reading context: **Continue reading**, **Kept passage**,
**Scores & past runs**. Reading prompts ask about books directly. No animal
dialogue describes imagined affection or recognition. Older animal narration in
the journal is presented neutrally without rewriting stored history.

The three games retain their scoring rules and saved runs:

- **Shelf Shift:** shows how many moves remain until the next bookmark choice.
- **Lantern Lines:** previews chain points and highlights every cleared symbol
  when a loop closes. Preview and committed moves share validation and scoring.
- **Book Balance:** shows the landing overlap, both alignment guides, and a
  stronger outline inside the perfect-landing tolerance.

Boards use the available screen height to leave room for controls. The current
reading title remains visible during a game. Daily boards, practice, ranked
history, reading-time accounting and existing local storage keys remain intact.

Implementation retains the older `Sable` and `Pica` function seams for callers and
persisted state; those names are not the companion's current product identity.

The current visual treatment uses a slim matte ribbon, a precise folded corner,
small ink apertures and restrained half-smiles. Continuous glances, blinks,
flexing and touch responses retain the same animation cadence. Reactions keep
the paper's proportions; style marks sit quietly near the foot of the ribbon.
The Classic Home cover uses its reserved column so the left-side mascot stays
visible at narrow widths. The revolving shelf keeps its existing three-book
selection and gesture controls.
