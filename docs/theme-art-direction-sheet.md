## The structure — and the one thing it changes

Every polarity is **six swatches: three primary, three supporting**.

| block | swatch 1 | swatch 2 | swatch 3 | supporting |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Light** | the **page** | the **ink** | the **signature accent** | three more accents |
| **Dark** | the **page** | a light accent | a second accent | three more accents |

**The finding that matters:** every light page on the sheet is a **cream near-white**
(`#EAE4D2`–`#FFF8EA`) and every dark page is a **near-black with only a whisper of tint**
(`#06121E`–`#12181E`). The theme does **not** live in the page. It lives in the **ink** — a
tinted near-black, `#172B3A` navy / `#211B32` violet / `#182A27` green / `#292A32` charcoal
— and in the **accents**.

That is the opposite of the direction the first curation pass took (saturated teal / violet
/ green pages). A page painted in the theme's own hue is exactly the "everything is
fundamentally the same colour" failure: nine pages, one per family, each a flat wash of its
own accent. The sheet instead gives nine *cream* rooms whose colour arrives as ink, as
accent, and as artwork.

## Where the live Kotlin disagrees

| family | sheet says | live `FolioArtDirection.kt` says | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ocean` light | page `#F4F1E8` cream, ink `#172B3A` | page `#F1FAF8` pale mint | page is tinted with the accent hue; should be cream |
| `jade` dark | page `#0F1412`, accents jade `#4ABF7B` + gold `#D5B66A` + slate `#6C8AA0` + rust `#C07863` | page `#160D18`, accents **magenta** `#E07BD7` + mint + periwinkle | **wrong family** — "Jade Noir" is jade + noir, not magenta |
| `solar` dark | page `#121318`, accents slate-blue `#3E5C8E` + ember `#E17A3F` | page `#080F1C`, accents gold + cyan + periwinkle | drifted |
| every light face | page is a cream near-white | page is tinted with the family's own hue | systematic |

The System pair (`gallery`: Light / Dark) is **not on the sheet** — it is the app's own
default, which is why it is the one place a truly neutral graphite page is right.
